The astrologer

A practice built over five decades

Sudhir Karmarkar began studying Vedic astrology in the early 1970s under traditional guidance in Nagpur, and has been reading charts professionally ever since. Over fifty years, his clients have ranged from families seeking a child's first kundli to couples weighing marriage to individuals navigating a difficult season of life.

His approach stays close to classical method — birth chart first, transits and dashas second, remedy only where it's genuinely called for. No generic printouts, no rushed calls: every reading is worked through by hand before it's shared.

How a consultation works

Simple to begin, thorough once you're in

Every reading — whether by phone or WhatsApp — follows the same unhurried process.

01

Share your details

Send date, time, and place of birth (and your partner's, for match making) over WhatsApp or phone.

02

Chart is prepared

Sudhirji casts and studies the kundli by hand before your call — this isn't read live off software.

03

A telephonic or WhatsApp voice conversation, at a time that suits you, going through what the chart shows.
